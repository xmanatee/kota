- **Dream-RSI:** the first link identifies
  [the primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14858v1).
  Section 3 evaluates branching and stopping policies against recorded discovery
  trees; replay reveals existing children and cannot generate unseen outcomes.
  Its objective includes solution quality, attempt cost and a parallelism bonus.
  The [author repository](https://github.com/zhengkid/Dream-RSI) still marks
  the codebase and discovered programs as being prepared. Added that evolving
  source to the weekly watchlist. Research question: can recorded KOTA outcomes
  predict a policy's performance on fresh work without selection bias? Existing
  workflow simulation previews event routing, and eval-harness explicitly does
  not support agent replay. This paper does not establish a reason to replace
  either owner or import its cost objective. Revisit with released replay data
  and a concrete KOTA policy comparison; no benchmark was executed.
