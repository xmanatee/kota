1. Preserved recovery cannot enter the builder until the checkpointed branch contains the recovery-time canonical head, or recovery is durably held for review.
2. One checkpoint preserves all tracked and untracked changes and records the original base, checkpoint commit, canonical head, conflicts, and disposition in claim/worktree evidence.
3. Non-overlapping divergence integrates automatically; overlapping paths alone reach the existing merge resolver, and task/source validation runs before continuation.
4. Canonical deletions and renames cannot be silently resurrected by resumed preserved work.
5. Concurrent canonical changes after continuation remain the final merge gate's responsibility without reprocessing pre-agent divergence.
6. Recovery status projections distinguish checkpointing, canonical reconciliation, conflict blocking, and readiness to resume.
